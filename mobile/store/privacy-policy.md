# Bakers Math privacy policy

_Last updated: [date you publish it]_

Bakers Math is an app for writing bread recipes, timing bakes and keeping a bake log. It stores
your data on a Bakers Math server run by you or by whoever gave you its address. The app's
developer does not run that server and does not receive your data.

## What the app stores

- Your email address and password (the password is stored hashed), to sign you in.
- The display name you choose, shown on recipes you make public.
- Your recipes, bakes, notes, ratings and step timings.
- Photos and videos you add to a bake.

This data is kept on your phone and on the server the app is connected to. Recipes you make public
and bakes you share can be seen by other people signed in to the same server.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, analytics or tracking.
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- No location, contacts or other data beyond what is listed above.

## Permissions

- Camera and microphone: only when you take a photo or video for a bake.
- Photo library: only when you pick a photo or video for a bake.
- Notifications: to alert you when a step's timer ends.
- Local network (iPhone): to reach a server on your home network.

## Deleting your data

Profile > Delete account removes your account, recipes, bakes, photos and videos from the server
and from your phone.
