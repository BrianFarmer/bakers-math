## Description [4000]

Bakers Math is a bread notebook that does the arithmetic for you.

Write a formula in grams or in baker's percentages and see both side by side: change one and the
other follows. A summary bar shows total dough weight, flour, hydration, salt and prefermented
flour as you type. The flour in your starter is kept out of the 100% base and still counted in
hydration, the way bakers work it out by hand.

Scale any recipe by total dough weight, by number of loaves, or by the flour you have.

Bake in guided mode, one step per screen. Each step can have a timer that keeps running with the
phone locked and alerts you with a sound when it ends. Pause, add five minutes, skip, or mark it
done. The screen stays on while you work.

Every bake goes into your bake log with its step timings, notes, a rating, and photos and videos
from the counter and the oven. Keep a bake private or share it on the recipe.

Everything works offline. Recipes and bakes sync with your own Bakers Math server when the phone
can reach it. Publish a recipe for others on your server to find, copy and download.

Bakers Math connects to a Bakers Math server that you run, such as one on a computer on your home
network. You enter its address when you sign in.

## App privacy answers

Data the app collects, all linked to the account and used only to run the app. Nothing is used
for tracking or ads, and nothing goes to third parties: it all lives on the server the user runs.

| Data | Why |
| ---- | --- |
| Email address | Sign in and password reset |
| Name | Shown on public recipes |
| Photos and videos | Attached to bakes by the user |
| Other user content (recipes, notes, ratings) | The app's purpose |

Data is encrypted in transit only when the server uses HTTPS (a home server on plain HTTP is
not). Users can delete their account and all its data in Profile > Delete account.
